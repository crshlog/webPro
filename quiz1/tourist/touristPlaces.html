Tourist Places

Alam Kandung

A little hidden within the natural forests on the eastern side of Tulungagung, there is a beautiful waterfall with an amazing view. You can swim in the shallow areas or simply listen to the sounds of nature as the water falls over the rocky mountains. Not far from here, there is also a place where the forest seems to open up, revealing a vast rice field stretching out beneath the mountains.

JLS Sine

a project to build a road connecting cities in the southern coast of java passing through Tulungagung, it has also brought an unexpected benefit making previously untouched parts of Tulungagung became some of the most beautiful areas in the region.road winding through with beautiful beaches along the way. making it a perfect place for a road trip, where not only the destination is beautiful, but the journey itself is just as enjoyable.

Gemah beach

a beach that stretches 2 km from end to end, with a unique natural landscape surrounded by cliffs. here, you can swim in the sea or simply relax with the gentle sea breeze and chat with your loved ones. if you want a little bit of adventure, you can also rent an atv and ride along the beach, enjoying the beautiful scenery along the way.

Sine Beach

Developed long before the JLS opened, this place was originally a fishing village with many fishing boats. Even after the JLS opened, it has retained its old fishing village atmosphere while also becoming a place for tourists to relax. You don't have to worry about food here, as there are plenty of local seafood stalls along the road. There is also a fish auction where you can buy fresh fish directly from local fishermen.

Wonorejo Dam

This dam is located at the foot of Mount Wilis or about 12 kilometers from downtown Tulungagung.
