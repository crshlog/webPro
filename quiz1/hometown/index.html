Tulungagung

My beloved hometown

located in the southern part of east java, this place is surrounded by amazing natural scenery, from beautiful beaches and simple mountains to refreshing waterfalls. it is neither a big city nor a small town, making it a perfect place to experience slow living once in a while—to relax, clear your mind, and enjoy peaceful days and quiet nights.

you probably already know about my hometown because of its marble production. in the southern part of the city, there is a limestone mountain range that contains a large amount of marble. naturally, the local residents make use of this resource not only to produce marble slabs, but also to create various handicrafts that can be used as decorations.

in the last few years, with the development of the java southern cross-island road, a lot of new beaches have become more accessible. there are many types of beautiful beaches that you can visit on a whim, as they are located relatively close to each other while still having their own unique characteristics. many cafes have also opened along the road, giving you plenty of places to relax, eat, or enjoy a refreshing drink. it is a perfect place for a summer vacation.
