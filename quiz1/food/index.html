Local Food

Ayam Lodho

ayam lodho is prepared by grilling or roasting free-range chicken until tender, then simmering it in a coconut milk-based sauce with various spices. the dish has a savory and spicy flavor, mainly coming from the coconut milk and aromatic spices used in its preparation. it is commonly served with sega gurih, or savory rice, which is similar to nasi uduk.

Cimplung

cimplung is a traditional dish commonly made from cassava, breadfruit, or other root vegetables. The ingredients are boiled until tender in water mixed with palm sugar and pandan leaves. The cooking process usually takes several hours until the cassava becomes soft and the palm sugar syrup becomes thick and sticky.

Gado gado

The term gado or the verb menggado means to consume something without rice.Gado-gado is made up of a rich mixture of vegetables, including potatoes, longbeans, bean sprouts, spinach, chayote, bitter gourd, corn and cabbage, along with tofu, tempeh, and hard-boiled eggs. They are all mixed in peanut sauce dressing, sometimes also topped with krupuk and sprinkles of fried shallots.

keripik gadung

keripik gadung is a traditional snack made from gadung tubers, which are thinly sliced, dried, and then fried. After being thinly sliced, the pieces may be coated with wood ash and then dried. They are then thoroughly washed under running water and dried again until completely dry before being fried. keripik gadung usually has a savory, salty taste with a pleasant garlic aroma.

Green Coffe

the coffee is called **kopi ijo** because it is ground together with mung beans until they become a fine powder. the resulting greenish-colored coffee is usually served on a small tray or plate. the fine coffee grounds are also commonly used for *nyethe*, a traditional practice of applying coffee paste to cigarettes because of its enjoyable taste. kopi ijo is also commonly bought as a traditional souvenir from tulungagung.

Jenang grendul

the glutinous rice balls have a chewy and soft texture, while the coconut milk sauce provides a well-balanced savory flavor. palm sugar adds a natural sweetness. this dish is best enjoyed warm in the morning or afternoon. it is also filling because it is made from glutinous rice, which is rich in carbohydrates. many people find a bowl of jenang grendul comforting and satisfying, as its taste can evoke nostalgic memories of childhood.
